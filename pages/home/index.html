Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can create an account?

Student, teacher, and school-admin accounts are created by a school administrator. Schools and companies get started by submitting a registration request from the "Register school/company" button — a super admin reviews it, verifies your details, and onboards your account. New accounts get in for the first time using the "Forgot your password?" link on the login page.

What happens after I submit a registration request?

Your request goes into a review queue for a super admin. They'll verify your details — by phone or email — before creating and activating your account, so there's a real person checking every new school or company that joins.

Can teachers upload notes and assignments?

Yes. Teachers can upload notes and assignments for a specific class and subject, and set deadlines for assignments.

How does the AI tutor work?

Students can paste a link to any note and ask the AI tutor to explain it, simplify it, or generate a practice question from it.

How do internship applications work?

A student sets their trade on their profile, applies to a matching internship, and their school admin approves the application and relays the registration fee before the company reviews and accepts them.

Can one internship posting cover more than one trade?

Yes. Companies can open a single posting to several trades at once — for example Software Development, Networking, and CSA together — instead of publishing a separate listing for each.

Who sets up classes, subjects, and trades?

School admins create classes and subjects and assign subjects to the teachers who will manage them; trades used for internship matching are managed the same way.

Is my data visible to other students?

No. Students only see their own submissions, results, and applications. Teachers see results for the classes and subjects they teach.
